Why guessing a discount costs more than you think

A discount comes out of your profit, not your sales. A product costs ₦50,000 and sells for ₦100,000, so you keep ₦50,000. You run a 30% flash sale.

₦70,000new selling price
₦20,000profit left (was ₦50,000)
-60%of your profit gone

To earn the same total profit at that price you must sell 2.5 times as many units. Before you cut a price, work out the floor.

Floor price = Total cost per unit ÷ (1 − Target margin) Max Safe Discount = 1 − Floor price ÷ Normal price

Margin here means profit as a share of the selling price. Total cost includes what you paid for the item plus packaging, delivery and payment fees for that sale.

3 ways to use your safe number in Q4

Tiered unlock

Give 10% off orders above one amount, 15% off above a higher amount, and keep the full safe discount for your highest order tier only.

Flash window

Run the deepest discount for a few hours only. Urgency brings the buyers, and the short window protects your normal price.

Bundle and save

Pair a high-margin item with a slow one and apply the discount to the bundle. Check the bundle with the calculator, not each item.

Before you launch any Q4 promo

1. Run each product you plan to discount through calculator 1.
2. Never publish a price below its floor.
3. Set your ad spend with calculator 2 before you boost a post.
4. Check sell-through every week. A product under 40% sold by week 4 needs action, not hope.
